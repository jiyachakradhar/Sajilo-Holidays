Ghandruk Tour

3 Days / 2 Nights •

About Trip

Ghandruk is one of Nepal’s most beautiful and culturally rich mountain villages, situated at an altitude of approximately 1,940 meters in the Annapurna region. Known for its traditional Gurung culture, stone-paved streets, terraced fields, and breathtaking views of the Annapurna and Machhapuchhre (Fishtail) ranges, Ghandruk offers a perfect combination of nature, culture, and relaxation. Enjoy spectacular sunrise and sunset views, and immerse themselves in the peaceful Himalayan surroundings.

Why this trip

Tour Highlights

  1. Muktinath Temple

    Darshan at 3,800m with the 108 sacred water spouts and the eternal flame.

  2. Marpha Village

    Stone-paved Thakali village known for its apple orchards and local brandy.

  3. Kali Gandaki Gorge

    Drive the world's deepest gorge, between Dhaulagiri and Annapurna.

  4. Rupse Waterfall

    A roadside stop at one of Nepal's most photogenic cascades.

  5. Thakali Cuisine

    Authentic Thakali thali served fresh in Muktinath and Marpha.

  6. Pokhara Lakeside

    Evenings by Phewa Lake, plus Davis Fall, Pumdikot and Gupteshwor Cave.
