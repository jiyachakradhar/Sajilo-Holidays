Chitlang Kulekhani

About Chitlang and Kulekhane

Chitlang and Kulekhani are among the most charming destinations near Kathmandu, offering a beautiful combination of ancient heritage, peaceful villages, lush forests, mountain landscapes, and serene lakes. Located in Makwanpur district, this region is ideal for travelers looking for a refreshing escape from the busy city.


Chitlang is believed to have a history dating back many centuries and is known for its traditional Newari settlements, stone-paved pathways, old houses, temples, and cultural heritage. The village provides an authentic glimpse into Nepal's traditional rural lifestyle.

Trip Highlights

  • Chitlang Countryside

    Explore Chitlang’s peaceful countryside, lush green hills, farms, and beautiful natural surroundings.

  • Newari Heritage

    Discover ancient Newari heritage, traditional village life, and historic cultural sites.

  • Sat Dhara

    Visit the scenic Sat Dhara and enjoy its peaceful natural surroundings.

  • Ashok Chaitya

    Explore Ashok Chaitya and other historic sites that reflect the region’s cultural heritage.

  • Kulekhani & Indra Sarovar

    Visit Kulekhani and Indra Sarovar, surrounded by lush green hills and scenic mountain landscapes.

Trip Itinerary, Day by Day

Two days from Kathmandu to Chitlang

Day one

Kathmandu to Chitlang

  • Assembly at Program Venue.
  • Drive towards Chitlang via Chandragiri.
  • Arrive at Chandragiri Deuarali and start Hiking to Bhaleshwor Temple
  • Descend back to Deurali and proceed to Chitlang.
  • Sightseeing at Satdhara (Seven Stone Spouts), Goat Cheese Factory, Chitlang Pear Orchards, Ashok chaitya and Traditional Newari Settlement
  • Enjoy campfire with music and entertainment.
Day two

Chitlang to Kathmandu

  • Enjoy the indra sarovar lake, sightseeing & optional boating.
  • Visit Bheda Farm.
  • Final departure for Kathmandu.
Day four

Pokhara to Kathmandu

  • Morning darshan at Taal Barahi Temple.
  • Breakfast at the hotel and time around the lakeside.
  • Check out and depart for Kathmandu.
  • Lunch at Muglin.
