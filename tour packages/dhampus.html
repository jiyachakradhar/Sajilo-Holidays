About Trip

Upper Mustang is a remote and culturally rich region of Nepal, famous for its dramatic Himalayan landscapes, ancient monasteries, traditional Tibetan-style villages, caves, and the historic walled city of Lo Manthang. The journey passes through the scenic Kali Gandaki Valley, Jomsom, Kagbeni, and the unique desert- like landscapes of Mustang.

Why this trip

Tour Highlights

  1. Muktinath Temple

    Darshan at 3,800m with the 108 sacred water spouts and the eternal flame.

  2. Marpha Village

    Stone-paved Thakali village known for its apple orchards and local brandy.

  3. Kali Gandaki Gorge

    Drive the world's deepest gorge, between Dhaulagiri and Annapurna.

  4. Rupse Waterfall

    A roadside stop at one of Nepal's most photogenic cascades.

  5. Thakali Cuisine

    Authentic Thakali thali served fresh in Muktinath and Marpha.

  6. Pokhara Lakeside

    Evenings by Phewa Lake, plus Davis Fall, Pumdikot and Gupteshwor Cave.

Trip Itinerary

Day 1

Kathmandu to Beni/Galeshwor

➢ Sightseeing places:

➢ Dinner/overnight at hotel.

Day 2

Beni-Muktinath-Chhusang

➢ Early morning Darshan at Galeshwor Mahadev Temple and breakfast at hotel.

➢ Departure for Muktinath Temple.

➢ Sightseeing Places:

• Rupse Waterfall

• KaagBeni

• Jomson

Day 3

Chhusang to Lomanthang

➢ Morning breakfast at the hotel.

• Korala Border

➢ Dinner/Overnight at hotel.

Day 4

Lo-Manthang–Marpha

Day 5
