Dhampus

About Dhampus Australian Camp

Dhampus is one of Nepal’s beautiful and peaceful mountain villages, situated at an altitude of approximately 1,650 meters in the Annapurna region. Known for its traditional Gurung culture, scenic landscapes, terraced fields, stone-paved paths, and spectacular views of the Annapurna and Machhapuchhre (Fishtail) ranges, Dhampus offers a perfect combination of nature, culture, adventure, and relaxation.

Trip Highlights

  • Panoramic Himalayan Views

    Enjoy breathtaking views of Annapurna South, Annapurna II, III and IV, Manaslu, Dhaulagiri, Machhapuchhre (Fishtail), and Hiunchuli.

  • Sunrise & Sunset Experience

    Witness spectacular sunrise and sunset views over the Himalayan peaks from Dhampus.

  • Traditional Gurung Village

    Explore the charming Gurung village of Dhampus and experience its rich culture and mountain lifestyle.

  • Scenic Countryside Journey

    Travel through picturesque hills, forests, and rural landscapes on a beautiful drive from Pokhara.

  • Terraced Fields & Nature

    Walk among lush terraced fields and enjoy the peaceful beauty of the Annapurna foothills.

  • Pokhara Exploration

    Discover Pokhara's major attractions, including Phewa Lake and other popular sightseeing spots.

Trip Itinerary, Day by Day

Three days from Kathmandu to Australian Base Camp and Pokhara

Day one

Kathmandu to Dhampus

  • Early morning drive from Kathmandu via Trishuli river.
  • Arrive at Dhampus and hike towards Australian Camp.
  • Enjoy spectacular views of the Mountain Range .
Day two

Dhampus to Pokhara

  • Early morning sunrise and Himalayan mountain-view experience.
  • Explore Dhampus village and nearby viewpoints.
  • Drive to Pokhara.
  • Visit Bindabasini Temple for darshan.
  • Pokhara sightseeing including Pumdikot, Davis Fall and Gupteshwor Cave.
  • Evening walk/hangout around Lakeside.
Day three

Pokhara to Kathmandu

  • Early morning visit to Talbahari Temple.
  • Start the drive to Kathmandu.
  • Tour concludes upon arrival in Kathmandu.
Day four

Pokhara to Kathmandu

  • Morning darshan at Taal Barahi Temple.
  • Breakfast at the hotel and time around the lakeside.
  • Check out and depart for Kathmandu.
  • Lunch at Muglin.
