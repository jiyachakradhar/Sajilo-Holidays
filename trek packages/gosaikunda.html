About Trip

Muktinath Temple is one of Nepal’s most sacred pilgrimage sites, located at an altitude of about 3,800 meters in the Himalayan region of Mustang. It is revered by both Hindus and Buddhists, who believe it to be a place of spiritual liberation and salvation. The temple is famous for its 108 water spouts and the eternal flame that burns naturally from underground gas.
