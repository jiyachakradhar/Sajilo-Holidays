Manang

About Manang

Manang is a stunning Himalayan village in northern Nepal, famous for its dramatic mountain landscapes, traditional villages, ancient monasteries, and unique Tibetan-influenced culture. Located at around 3,500 meters, it is a popular destination for adventure, nature, and mountain lovers. Manang also offers spectacular views of the Annapurna and Gangapurna ranges.

Trip Itinerary, Day by Day

Five days from Kathmandu to Manang and back, with nights in Tal Village, Manang and Besisahar.

Day one

Kathmandu to Tal Village

  • Early morning departure from Kathmandu.
  • Scenic drive through the Prithvi Highway towards Besisahar.
  • Lunch en route.
  • Continue via Besisahar, Jagat, Dharapani and Chyamche towards Tal.
  • Enjoy breathtaking views of waterfalls, forests, rivers and Himalayan landscapes.
  • Arrival at Tal Village, hotel check-in, dinner and overnight stay.
Day two

Tal Village to Manang

  • Early morning breakfast at the hotel.
  • Drive from Tal Village towards Manang through the spectacular Marsyangdi Valley.
  • Visit the beautiful Blue Lake and Green Lake along the route.
  • Lunch en route.
  • Enjoy the surrounding mountain landscapes and traditional settlements.
  • Arrival in Manang, hotel check-in, dinner and overnight stay.
Day three

Explore Manang

  • Early morning breakfast at the hotel.
  • Explore Manang Village and experience its traditional Himalayan lifestyle.
  • Visit Braga Village and ancient Buddhist monasteries.
  • Enjoy breathtaking views of the Annapurna and Gangapurna ranges.
  • Visit Gangapurna Lake and the glacier viewpoint.
  • Free time for photography and relaxation before returning to the hotel.
Day four

Manang to Besisahar

  • Early morning breakfast at the hotel.
  • Depart from Manang towards Besisahar.
  • Enjoy the scenic drive through the Marsyangdi Valley.
  • Lunch en route.
  • Continue the journey towards Besisahar through the beautiful mountain and river landscapes.
  • Arrival at Besisahar, hotel check-in and overnight stay.
Day five

Besisahar to Kathmandu

  • Breakfast at the hotel.
  • Depart from Besisahar for Kathmandu.
  • Enjoy scenic views along the Marsyangdi and Trishuli River valleys.
  • Lunch en route.
  • Arrival in Kathmandu.
  • End of the memorable Manang–Tal Village tour.
